Our Approach

Cloth cut with intent

A well-made agbada is an architecture of its own — the drape of the sleeve, the weight of the embroidery, the way the boubou falls when you sit versus when you stand. We treat every commission as a small structural problem before it's a fashion one.

Each client's measurements, preferences, and past fittings are kept on file, so a return visit for a wedding, a chieftaincy event, or a Friday jummah kaftan starts from where we left off, not from zero.
